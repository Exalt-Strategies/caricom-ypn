06 · Rules

Do / Don't

Do
  • Clash colors hard between neighbouring layers
  • Round everything · inner less than outer
  • Thick 12px photo frames, always
  • Even floating gutters; nothing touches the edge
  • Wordmark on top, near full width
Don't
  • No gradients, shadows, or glassmorphism
  • Never two same-family colors touching
  • No thin/light type or serifs
  • No full-bleed photos
  • No muted or pastel-only palettes
