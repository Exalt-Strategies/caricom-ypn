The Caribbean Young Parliamentarians Network launches this year, convened by the Centre for Youth Policy.

It begins as a simple idea: the youngest members in the region's parliaments should not be doing the work alone. The network brings them together across parties and across borders, so experience, advice and a shared agenda can travel between chambers that rarely talk to each other between sittings.
