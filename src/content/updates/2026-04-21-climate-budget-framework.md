Six national chapters have signed on to a shared framework for scrutinising
climate and resilience spending: young members read the relevant budget lines,
question the responsible minister, and publish a short public note before the
vote.

The point is not to slow budgets down. It is to make sure the people who will
live longest with a coastline decision have read the line item that pays for
it.

The framework is deliberately light: one template, one meeting, one published
note per budget cycle. Chapters in three more states are considering it.
