The newest national chapter held its first meeting this week, which means all
fifteen member parliaments of the Caribbean Community now have young members
organised inside the network.

Five years ago this was four people talking in a corridor between sessions.
The milestone matters less as a number than as reach: a priority agreed by the
regional council can now be carried into every chamber in the region at once.

The regional council marked it quietly, then went back to the order paper.
There is a first-job bill to move.
