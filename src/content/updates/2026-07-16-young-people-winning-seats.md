Across the Caribbean, a new cohort of young people is winning office.

In 2025, Roshanna Trim (born 1995) took a seat in the Senate of Barbados, and Kareem Marcelle (born 1995) was elected to represent Laventille West in Trinidad and Tobago. They are part of a wider shift the [Inter-Parliamentary Union](https://www.ipu.org/news/news-in-brief/2024-08/who-are-youngest-and-oldest-parliamentarians-in-world) has been tracking worldwide. The network exists to connect members like these to one another.
