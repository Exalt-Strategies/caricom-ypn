title: "Reserved committee-seat rule passes in Guyana"
date: 2026-03-12
tag: win
excerpt: "Guyana's National Assembly adopts the network's standing rule reserving committee places for members under forty."
image: "/img/updates/update-parliament.jpg"
---

The National Assembly in Georgetown adopted the reserved-seat rule this week,
becoming the first chamber to write the network's flagship priority into its
own standing orders.

The language is deliberately plain: every select committee must offer at least
one place to a sitting member under forty. It was carried by members from three
different parties, which is exactly how the network wants these things to move.

Chapters in four other states have asked for the drafting notes. The regional
council will circulate a model amendment next month.
