As it launches, the network is organising around a small set of priorities its members care about.

A stronger voice for younger members on the committees that shape budgets and reform. Bringing young parliamentarians into the scrutiny of climate and resilience spending. And championing the school-to-first-job pathway young Caribbean people keep asking for. These are starting points, to be shaped by the members themselves.
