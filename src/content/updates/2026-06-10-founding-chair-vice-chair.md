The network's founding members have chosen a chair and vice chair to steer its first year.

Senator Brittney Galvez of Belize serves as founding chair, with Senator Jonathan Wehner of Antigua and Barbuda as vice chair. Their job in year one is straightforward: help the network find its feet, bring more young members in, and set out how it will be run.
