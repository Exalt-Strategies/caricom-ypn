---
title: "Jamaica chapter opens with nine members"
date: 2026-04-02
tag: chapter
excerpt: "Nine young members across both houses form the network's largest national chapter to date."
---

The Jamaica chapter launched in Kingston this week with nine sitting members
drawn from both the House of Representatives and the Senate, the largest
national chapter the network has stood up so far.

A chapter is not a social club. Its first job is to carry the network's shared
priorities onto the order paper of its own parliament, and to run training for
the next cohort of candidates before the next general election.

The chapter's first working session set two targets: a youth place on the
public accounts committee, and a candidate school before the year is out.
