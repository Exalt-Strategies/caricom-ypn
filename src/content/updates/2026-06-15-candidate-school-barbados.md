---
title: "Candidate school in Barbados trains 40 hopefuls"
date: 2026-06-15
tag: event
excerpt: "Forty aspiring members spent a weekend in Bridgetown learning the mechanics of running and winning."
---

Forty aspiring candidates spent a weekend in Bridgetown at the network's
largest candidate school yet, taught by members who have already run, won, and
in a few honest sessions, lost.

The curriculum is unglamorous on purpose: how a nomination actually works,
how to read a constituency, how to canvass without a budget, and how a first
budget cycle feels from the inside.

Every attendee left with a mentor in a neighbouring chapter and an open
invitation to shadow a sitting member through a full week of the parliamentary
calendar.
