---
title: "First-job guarantee bill picks up four sponsors"
date: 2026-05-27
tag: campaign
excerpt: "A model bill on the school-to-first-job pathway now has sponsors lined up in four parliaments."
---

The network's model bill on a graduate first-job pathway now has committed
sponsors in four parliaments, the first time one of our drafts will be
introduced in more than one chamber in the same session.

The bill is written to be adapted, not copied: it sets a common goal, a paid
first role within a year of leaving school or training, and leaves each state
to fund and phase it in its own way.

Sponsors will coordinate their first readings so the debates can reference one
another. The drafting notes go to every chapter this week.
